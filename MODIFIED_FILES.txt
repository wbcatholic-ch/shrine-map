  .rs-waypoint6-drag-slot ~ .rs-waypoint10-drag-slot

  .rs-waypoint6-end-tools ~ .rs-waypoint10-end-tools
  를 사용하고 있었음

  6=13, 7=15, 8=17, 9=19, 10=21

  15,17,19,21,23 행까지 확장
